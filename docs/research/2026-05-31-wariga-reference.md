## 1. SOLID — high confidence, usable for educational copy (with attribution)

**1.1 Calendar mechanics.** Two parallel calendars: the **sasih** (Saka) lunar calendar (12 lunar months) and the **Pawukon**, an arithmetic **210-day** cycle = least common multiple of the 5/6/7-day cycles; expressible as **30 wuku** or 6 × 35-day months. Up to **ten concurrent "weeks" (eka–dasa wara)** run at once, so every day carries several names; the 4/8/9-day weeks need repeated days to fit.
_Sources:_ Cambridge UP _Calendrical Calculations_ (Reingold & Dershowitz, peer-reviewed, **primary**); IJLLC (peer-reviewed); Wikipedia (corroboration). Vote 3-0.

**1.2 What Wariga / dewasa ayu is.** A traditional Balinese-Hindu knowledge system (rooted in Jyotisha + the _Rwa Bhineda_ dualism), transmitted via lontar, oral tradition and ritual; its purpose (_padewasan_) is choosing good vs bad days. _"Ala ayuning dewasa"_ = ala (not good) + ayu (good). **Dewasa ayu** = an auspicious day; **ala dewasa** = inauspicious. Applied to yadnya, marriage, construction, business, agriculture, travel.
_Sources:_ JPAH 2025 (SINTA-2 peer-reviewed, STAHN Mpu Kuturan Singaraja, **primary**); UPI museum (state-university, **primary**). Vote 3-0.

**1.3 The elements Wariga reads.** dauh (time-of-day), penanggal (waxing date), panglong (waning date), wewaran (eka–dasa wara), pawukon (30 wuku), sasih (12 months), warsa (year). Each component carries a "life value" (**urip/neptu**) and is believed to belong to particular deities.
_Sources:_ UPI museum (**primary**); Cambridge UP (**primary**). Vote 3-0.
_Flag:_ the **count** of top-level components varies by source (5 vs 6 vs 7) — names are consistent; some add "dauh"/"manah hning"/omit "warsa". Hedge the taxonomy.

**1.4 Sasih mechanics.** A sasih = 30 lunar days: **15 penanggal** (waxing, suklapaksa) before the full moon + **15 panglong/pangelong** (waning, kresnapaksa) before the new moon (**Tilem**); **Purnama** (full moon) is broadly regarded as a holy/auspicious time.
_Sources:_ IJLLC (peer-reviewed); Wikipedia. Vote 3-0.
_Flag:_ not every Purnama is a formally-scored dewasa ayu (the full padewasan combines sasih+wuku+wewaran).

**1.7 Authority & sourcing (basis for the disclaimer).** The authoritative knowledge sits with a recognised **padewasan expert / Sulinggih / Pemangku** or an **authoritative lontar / printed book**. **PHDI** is the recognised Hindu body, but its official pages carry **no Wariga content** — citable only for its _identity_, not for mechanics or rules. Open-web sources are **insufficient** for precise per-ceremony rules.
_Sources:_ Jurnal Lontar Komputer (Udayana, 2014, **primary**); parisada.or.id (**primary**, verified: org history only). Vote 3-0. → Directly supports our "pedoman umum / not affiliated PHDI" stance.

## 2. CAUTIOUS — medium confidence, publish only with hedging

**2.1 How a day is judged (general principle).** A commonly-cited axiom ranks elements — _"wewaran alah dening wuku, wuku alah dening penanggal/panglong, … alah dening sasih …"_ (sasih outranks wuku); and popularly, a day is good if it holds more **dewa** elements, bad if more **raksasa/kala** — with the operational counterpart being a numerical **urip balance (Lanus/Basah)**.
_Sources:_ Vidyottama Sanatana 2019 (peer-reviewed, for "alahing sasih"); Denpasar city-gov radio quoting an IHDN lecturer (dewa/raksasa); kalenderbali.info (axiom text). Mixed votes.
_Caveat:_ the **full fixed override hierarchy as one ordered chain was REFUTED (1-2)** — present the ranking as a _commonly-cited axiom that varies_, not settled mechanics. Present dewa/raksasa as the _popular_ explanation, Lanus/Basah as its computational counterpart.

**2.2 Considerations differ by ceremony (Panca Yajña + "nunas dewasa").** The classical baseline is the **five Panca Yajña** (Dewa / Pitra / Manusa / Rsi / Bhuta Yajña), each with its own padewasan. Documented examples: **pawiwahan** day from the couple's **weton**; **Manusa Yajña** judged on _alahing sasih_; **pitra yadnya (ngaben)** from pawukon + sasih + Hindu astrology.
_Sources:_ Vidyottama Sanatana 2019 (peer-reviewed, the principle); JPAH 2025 (the per-ceremony specifics); Jurnal Lontar Komputer 2014 (cultural weight of the wedding day).
_Caveat:_ the **detailed per-ceremony mechanics come from a SINGLE Buleleng ethnography** → cite as **localized regional practice, not all-Bali fact**.
